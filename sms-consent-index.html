SMS Consent & Opt-In Process

How businesses using Netify Builds collect SMS consent from their customers

Netify Builds provides a Reactivation Campaign service that allows local service businesses (HVAC, pest control, landscaping, pool service, and similar industries) to send seasonal SMS reminders to their own past customers. This page documents exactly how consent is collected before any customer is added to a messaging list.

Important: Netify Builds does not collect consent directly from end consumers. Consent is collected by our business clients at the point of service, using the process described below. Businesses confirm they have an existing relationship and proper consent from every contact before submitting a list to us.

How Consent Is Collected

A customer hires a local service business (for example, an HVAC company) for a repair, installation, or maintenance visit.
During the service visit or checkout process, the business collects the customer's name and phone number on an intake form, invoice, or service agreement.
The form includes a clear, unchecked consent checkbox (shown in the mockup below) that the customer must actively check to opt in to text message reminders.
Only customers who checked this box are included in the business's list submitted to Netify Builds for seasonal reactivation texts.
Every message sent includes a clear STOP opt-out instruction, honored immediately and permanently.

Required Disclosures

Every opt-in checkbox used by our business clients must include the following disclosures, matching the language shown in the mockup above:

  • Clear statement that the customer is agreeing to receive text messages
  • "Message and data rates may apply"
  • Message frequency disclosure (e.g. "varies" or a specific cadence)
  • Instructions to reply STOP to opt out
  • Instructions to reply HELP for assistance
  • A link to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Opt-Out Handling

Any recipient may reply STOP at any time to immediately and permanently unsubscribe from all future messages. Recipients may reply START to a message to re-subscribe, or HELP for assistance. All opt-out requests are processed automatically and honored without delay.

Business Client Certification

Before any customer list is accepted for a reactivation campaign, the business client must confirm in writing (via our Terms of Service) that:

  • Every contact on the submitted list is an existing customer who has provided consent using a process consistent with the one described on this page
  • No purchased, rented, scraped, or cold contact lists will be submitted
  • The business accepts full legal responsibility for the compliance of any list they submit
